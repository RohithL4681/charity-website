## What we do

Healthcare costs push many families into debt. Our camps bring basic care to the community itself:

- **Monthly check-up camps** with doctors and nurses
- **Free medicines** dispensed on the spot
- **Screening drives** for blood pressure, diabetes and anaemia
- **Awareness sessions** on hygiene, nutrition and preventive care

## Our reach

In the last year, **2,000+ people** received free consultations, and 150 seniors with chronic conditions were enrolled in follow-up monitoring.
