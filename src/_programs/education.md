## What we do

Every year we identify children at risk of dropping out of school — usually because their family cannot afford supplies, tuition or transport. We step in with:

- **Full scholarships** for tuition and exam fees
- **School kits** with books, uniforms and stationery every term
- **Learning centres** providing free after-school support and homework help
- **Mentorship** pairing each child with a volunteer mentor

## Our reach

Last year, **1,500 children** stayed in school thanks to this program, with a 94% pass rate among scholarship students.
