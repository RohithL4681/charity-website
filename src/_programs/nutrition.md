## What we do

Hunger is invisible until it isn't. Our nutrition program works quietly every week to make sure no one in our communities goes to bed hungry:

- **Weekly meal kits** for 800+ families, containing staples like rice, dal, pulses, oil and spices
- **Daily midday meals** for children at our learning centres
- **Elder care packs** delivered to seniors living alone

## Our reach

Over the past year we distributed **35,000+ meals**, including a special drive during the winter that reached 400 families.
