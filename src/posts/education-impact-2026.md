This year's education report is out, and the numbers are worth celebrating.

**Highlights:**

- **1,500 children** supported through scholarships and school kits
- **94% pass rate** among our sponsored students
- **18 new mentors** joined, giving 300 children weekly one-to-one support

## A story from the field

Asha (name changed) is 14 and the eldest of four. When her father lost work, she was close to quitting school to take up domestic work. Our team enrolled her in the scholarship program, provided her kits, and paired her with a mentor.

Today, Asha is back in class and stood first in her unit test this term.

## The road ahead

Next year we want to raise that number to 2,000 children. It costs roughly ₹1,000 a month to keep one child in school. [Support the program](/donate/) and help us get there.
