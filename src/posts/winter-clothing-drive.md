This December, with the help of 60 volunteers and hundreds of donors like you, we organised our biggest winter drive yet.

**What happened:**

- 400 families received warm clothing, blankets and sweaters
- 120 of those families were recommended by their local schools
- We partnered with two local tailors who stitched 150 new woollen blankets

> "I cannot describe the warmth — not just of the clothes, but of knowing people who don't even know me cared for my kids." — Sunita, mother of three

## How you helped

Every ₹500 donation funded fresh blankets for two children. Your generosity is the only reason this was possible.
